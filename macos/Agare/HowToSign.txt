Agare — if Set up with Xcode said “No Account for Team”

The helper only uses teams listed under Xcode → Settings → Accounts.
A leftover “Apple Development” item in Keychain is ignored on purpose.

1. Xcode → Settings (⌘,) → Accounts
2. Select your Apple ID. If it looks expired, sign out and sign in again.
3. Under that account a Team must appear (your name = free personal team).
4. Click Set up with Xcode again in the Agare window.

The helper then stamps that Team on both Agare and AgareExtension and
signs with “Apple Development” (not the old “Mac Development” certificate).
