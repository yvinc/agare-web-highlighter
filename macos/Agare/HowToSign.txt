Agare — automatic Personal Team

Set up with Xcode:
  1. macOS may show “Terminal wants to control this computer”. Click Allow.
     Agare cannot flip Accessibility on without that popup.
  2. The helper opens Signing & Capabilities, selects your name
     (Personal Team) — not red Unknown Name — saves, and builds both targets.

If Team is still red Unknown Name, click your name (Personal Team) once,
then run Set up with Xcode again.
